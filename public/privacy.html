leetzone

Privacy

Covers the LeetZone site and the LeetZone browser extension. Last updated 5 October 2026.

LeetZone is a small game for playing LeetCode against friends, run by Aditya Agrawal. It collects only what it needs to run lobbies and keep score, never sells or shares it, and shows no ads.

What the site keeps

  • The display name you choose.
  • A login token the server creates for you. Your browser keeps the token; the server stores only a one-way hash of it.
  • Lobbies and matches: who played, which LeetCode problems were picked, and for each of your judged submissions to those problems its verdict, how many test cases passed, the language, and when it arrived. Scores are computed from these.

Names, scores and match results are visible to other players and on the leaderboard and profile pages.

What the extension does

  • On leetcode.com, it watches the responses LeetCode sends back when you submit a solution and reads the verdict: problem, status, test cases passed, language and submission number.
  • Only while you are in a LeetZone lobby, it sends those results to the LeetZone server with your login token, and shows the match clock and scoreboard on the page. The server ignores results for problems that are not an open question in your match.
  • On the LeetZone site, it copies your LeetZone session (name, login token, current lobby) into the extension's own storage, so it can report for you. It also remembers where you put its panel.

It never reads or sends your code, your LeetCode account details or password, or anything from other websites. It talks only to the LeetZone server.

Who else sees it

LeetZone runs on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to serve and protect the site, and keeps request logs for a few days. No analytics, trackers, advertising or other third parties. The site sets no cookies.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept while LeetZone runs. Signing out on the site removes your session from that browser and from the extension. To have your player and results deleted, email aditya.agrawal.a.777@gmail.com.

Changes

If what LeetZone collects changes, this page and the date above will say so before the change ships, and the extension will tell you.
